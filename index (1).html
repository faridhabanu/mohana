Technology Overview

Why Li-Fi?

💡

Visible Light Comm

Utilizes LED light modulation at frequencies imperceptible to the human eye to encode binary data streams in real-time.

⚡

Ultra High Speed

Delivers data rates up to 224 Gbps — 100× faster than conventional Wi-Fi by leveraging the vast optical spectrum.

🔒

Secure Channel

Light cannot penetrate walls, providing inherent physical security — no signal leakage, no eavesdropping.

📡

No RF Pollution

Ideal for hospitals, aircraft, and sensitive environments where RF interference must be eliminated entirely.

🌱

Energy Efficient

LED lighting infrastructure doubles as a data transmission medium — reducing additional hardware overhead significantly.

🏭

Industrial IoT

Enables reliable, interference-free machine-to-machine communication in dense industrial automation environments.

Real-World Applications

🏥

Healthcare

Safe communication in ICUs, OTs, and MRI rooms where RF is hazardous to sensitive medical equipment.

✈️

Aviation

In-flight entertainment and cabin communications without RF interference to navigation systems.

🏭

Industry 4.0

Factory automation with interference-free machine-to-machine optical data links.

🏫

Smart Classrooms

High-bandwidth internet to every desk through ceiling LED panels — no Wi-Fi congestion.

🚗

Vehicular Comms

V2V and V2I communication using traffic lights and headlamps for low-latency data exchange.

🌊

Underwater Comms

Blue-green light penetrates water far better than RF — enabling deep-sea IoT networks.

Project Info

  • Platform: Arduino / ESP32
  • Modulation: OOK / OFDM / PPM
  • Range: Up to 10 meters
  • Frequency: 400–700 THz
